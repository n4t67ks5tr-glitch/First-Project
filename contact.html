Frequently asked questions

How fast does shipping take?

Orders ship within 1–2 business days and typically arrive in 3–5 business days via standard ground shipping. Shipping is free on orders over $35.

What's your return policy?

You have 30 days from delivery to return any item for a full refund. Unused, resalable condition preferred, but we handle case-by-case exceptions — just reach out.

Do you restock sold-out items?

Yes — most items restock weekly. Subscribe to our newsletter for restock alerts on specific products.

Can I change or cancel an order after placing it?

Orders can be changed or canceled within 1 hour of purchase. After that, they've usually entered packing — contact us and we'll do what we can.

Is this a real store I can buy from?

Burrow & Bolt is a demo storefront built to showcase a niche household-goods shopping experience. The cart and checkout flow work, but no real payment is collected.
