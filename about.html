✦ Our story

We sell the stuff you didn't know you'd need — until you needed it at 9pm on a Tuesday.

Burrow & Bolt started with a broken cabinet hinge and a drawer with no working screwdriver in it. We built the store we wished existed: a small, honest catalog of the niche household items people actually use every day, without wading through a warehouse of things they don't.

Why we're narrow on purpose

Fewer, better choices

Most stores expand endlessly. We do the opposite. Every product on Burrow & Bolt earns its spot by solving one specific, recurring household annoyance — the wobbly chair leg, the grout line that never gets clean, the drawer of mismatched hangers.

When we add something new, we're replacing a worse version of itself, not just adding SKUs. That's why the catalog stays small: 24 products, not 24,000.
